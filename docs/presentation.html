06 / The Core Rule

Cash Flow Beats Spreadsheet Spread

SPREADSHEET FANTASY
“12% - 8% = 4% Free Profit”
Assumes markets move in a smooth, continuous straight line. Ignores market drawdowns and forced selling.
MARKET REALITY
“EMI Arrives Every 1st of the Month”
Markets crash unpredictably. Rigid monthly bills force you to liquidate assets at the worst possible time.

Debt needs certainty.
Compounding needs time.

Never make a volatile asset pay a rigid bill.
