# Cash Buy vs Loan + Invest — Debunk Video Script (Improvised)

**Format:** 16:9 interactive motion presentation / video  
**Runtime:** ~90–100 seconds  
**Tone:** engaging hook, clear visual storytelling, eye-opening reality check  
**Core thesis:** A spreadsheet assumes a smooth 12% return. Real markets swing violently while EMI arrives on the 1st of every month. Forcing a volatile asset to pay a fixed debt destroys compounding.

---

## Verified Backtest Data (Sep 2007 – Aug 2026 / 227 Months)

- **Scenario:** Buyer has ₹50 Lakh cash in hand for a home.
- **Finfluencer Pitch:** Pay ₹10L down payment (20%), take ₹40L home loan, invest remaining ₹40L cash in Nifty 50 TRI (~12%), and let monthly SWP pay the loan. “Free house + leftover profit!”
- **Realized Nifty 50 TRI CAGR:** 10.79% (started at 885 units)
- **Baseline (Untouched ₹40L in Nifty):** **₹2.77 Crore** (`₹2,77,73,804`)
- **Best Case (6.5% loan | EMI ₹30,663):** **₹31.54 Lakh** left (100.5 units / 88% units sold)
- **Normal Case (8.0% loan | EMI ₹34,244):** **₹2.79 Lakh** left (8.9 units / 99% units vaporized)
- **The Culprit:** The 2008 crash (Sequence of Returns Risk). Rigid EMI forced fire-sales at market bottoms.
